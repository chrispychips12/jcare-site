What we help with

Everyday life, your way.

For self managed, plan managed and private clients across Campbelltown, Liverpool, Bankstown and surrounds.

Which plans can we take?

We work with self managed and plan managed NDIS participants, and privately funded clients. We're not an NDIS registered provider, so we can't take agency managed plans.

  • Personal care

    Showering, bathing and toileting support, in and out of home. Post-operative care, dressing, and help moving about.

  • Help around the house

    Cleaning, washing and ironing, groceries, meal preparation, and keeping the garden in order.

  • Life skills

    Morning, afternoon and evening routines. Cooking and meal prep, bills and paperwork, medication prompts, and help understanding the NDIS.

  • Social interaction and community

    Joining social groups and meeting people. Parks, entertainment and dining out, plus dance, art, yoga and music therapy.

  • Therapy support

    Getting to and from appointments, and planning and booking occupational therapy, hydrotherapy, speech therapy and sports psychology. Then carrying the therapy on at home and in the community.

  • Transport and driver services

    Driver assisted transport to work, school, therapy or day services. Airport shuttles, social group tours, day trips and van hire.

How we work

What we owe you.

Most providers list what they expect of staff. These are our promises to you.

  • People depend on us

    You know who is coming, and when.

    We don't take on work we can't staff properly, and we don't move the roster once it's out.

  • Follow their lead

    Your routine, your way.

    If you have a particular way of doing something, we learn it properly rather than doing it ours.

  • Say it early, take it well

    You hear it the same day.

    If something seems different, we tell you that day. When you tell us how you want things done, that's information, not criticism.

  • Do it properly

    The same standard, watched or not.

    Notes written the same shift. Checks and certificates kept current.

For support workers

Work with one person, close to home.

Ongoing, casual roles across South West Sydney, paid at SCHADS award rates (MA000100) with higher rates for evenings and weekends.

Apply below

  • Same client, not a new house every shift
  • SCHADS award rates
  • Work close to home
What you'll need before your first shift
  • NDIS Worker Screening Check, or you're willing to get one before you start
  • National Police Check from the last twelve months
  • Current First Aid and CPR
  • If the role involves driving: a full licence, plus registration and comprehensive insurance that covers work use
  • Right to work in Australia

A Certificate III in Individual Support or Disability helps, but it isn't decisive. Some of our best workers started without one. What matters is patience, reliability and being someone a client trusts in their home.
